CoFarmer brings professional agricultural IoT monitoring to your farm. Connect to your CoFarmer hub to monitor sensors, climate systems, and equipment in real-time. Built for greenhouses, open fields, vertical farms, vineyards, and more.

CoFarmer puts powerful monitoring tools in your hands:
- Real-time sensor data - Monitor temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and other critical parameters from your agricultural installation.
- Alert on important thresholds - Get notified when conditions drift outside optimal ranges to protect your crops and equipment.
- Local hub architecture - Your CoFarmer hub runs on your farm. Data stays local for security and reliability, with optional cloud access when needed.
- Works offline - Designed for agricultural environments. Core monitoring works even without internet connectivity.
- Multiple installation support - Manage several farms or greenhouses from one app.
- Secure data control - Your operational data belongs to you. No third-party analytics or tracking.

Smartphone and tablet integration:
- Monitor from anywhere - Check your installation status remotely when away from the farm.
- Automatic local network detection - The app finds your CoFarmer hub automatically when on-site.
- Background sensors - Optional phone sensor integration for additional environmental monitoring.
- Smart notifications - Critical alerts go through when you need them, with full control over what triggers notifications.
- Quick widgets - Check installation status at a glance from your home screen.
- Wear OS support - Monitor your installation from your smartwatch.

Built by agricultural professionals, for agricultural professionals. CoFarmer is open source and transparent about how your data is handled.

Supported sensors and systems: Temperature, Humidity, Soil Moisture, HVAC Systems, Coolers, Heaters, Climate Control, Irrigation Systems, Nutrient Monitoring, Lighting Control, Air Circulation, Dehumidification, CO2 Monitoring, Power Monitoring, and more.
